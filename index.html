Common Questions

What should I wear?

Whatever you’re comfortable arriving in. You’ll have privacy to undress to your preferred level; draping is always secure.

Is the pressure gentle?

Always within your comfort. No “push through it” work — we check in and stay where your body can stay relaxed.

Is it good for first-timers?

Yes. We start with a short chat, then set the pace, pressure and ritual around how you feel that day.

Cancellation policy?

We ask for 24 hours notice for changes so we can offer the hour to another guest.
